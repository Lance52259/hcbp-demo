# Introduction

## What is Advanced Anti-DDoS (AAD)

Advanced Anti-DDoS (AAD) is a professional DDoS protection service provided by Huawei Cloud, designed to protect Internet servers and applications from distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks and other malicious traffic. AAD provides comprehensive protection capabilities, including DDoS traffic cleaning, CC (Challenge Collapsar) attack protection, and intelligent traffic analysis, ensuring the availability and stability of online services.

AAD supports multiple access modes, including website access and IP access, and can flexibly adapt to different business scenarios. At the same time, AAD provides elastic bandwidth expansion, protection package upgrade, and other capabilities, helping users dynamically adjust protection policies according to business needs and reduce security operation costs.

AAD also provides black/white list management functions. Users can add malicious IP addresses to the blacklist for blocking and trusted IP addresses to the whitelist for allowing, achieving fine-grained access control and further improving the security protection level of their business.
