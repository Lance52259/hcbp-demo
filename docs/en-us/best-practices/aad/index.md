# Introduction

## What is Advanced Anti-DDoS (AAD)

Advanced Anti-DDoS (AAD) is a professional DDoS protection service provided by Huawei Cloud. It is designed to protect Internet servers and applications from distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks and other malicious traffic. AAD provides comprehensive protection capabilities, including DDoS traffic cleaning, CC (Challenge Collapsar) attack protection, and intelligent traffic analysis, ensuring the availability and stability of your online services.

AAD offers flexible deployment modes to meet different business requirements. You can choose between website access and IP access modes, and select from multiple resource regions such as North China, East China, and Asia Pacific. The service supports both IPv4 and IPv6, and provides various bandwidth options including basic, elastic, and service bandwidth to accommodate different traffic volumes. Additionally, AAD integrates with enterprise project management, allowing you to associate instances with specific enterprise projects for better resource governance.

With AAD, you can effectively mitigate the impact of DDoS attacks, maintain service continuity, and protect your brand reputation. The service provides real-time attack monitoring and alerting, enabling you to respond quickly to security threats. By leveraging AAD's black and white list features, you can precisely control which IP addresses are allowed or blocked, further enhancing your security posture.
